THE METHOD

Image context. Language guidance.

A ConvNeXt image encoder and CXR-BERT text encoder guide the existing LanGuideMedSeg decoder. The Flask interface reuses the validated Phase-2 pipeline and preserves input alignment. Predictions are research outputs, not diagnostic conclusions.
